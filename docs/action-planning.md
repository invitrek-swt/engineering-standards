## Action Planning Workflow (Org-Level Standard)

Before any implementation, contributors must prepare and confirm a Plan of Actions:

1. **Context framing** – Define scope, actors, dependencies, and applicable org standards.
2. **Feature discovery** – Collect maximum possible features (core, extended, roadmap).
3. **Plan draft** – Outline execution steps, risks, and dependencies.
4. **Confirmation loop** – Present plan for review and refine until approved.
5. **Execution** – Begin implementation only after confirmation.
